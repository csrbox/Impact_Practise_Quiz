Social Stock Exchange (SSE)

Zero Coupon Zero Principal (ZCZP) Instruments

SEBI & MCA Regulatory Framework for Social Sector Finance

What is a ZCZP?

Zero Coupon Zero Principal instruments are specialized securities listed on the Social Stock Exchange (SSE). They carry zero interest and zero principal repayment, acting as formal, audited philanthropic grants for Non-Profit Organizations (NPOs).

Why are CSR Foundations Subscribing?

Subscription to ZCZPs guarantees standardized exchange disclosures, audited fund utilization, mandatory Social Audit reports, and verifiable outcome tracking, eliminating reporting opacity.

Is it Eligible Under Indian CSR Law?

Yes! The Ministry of Corporate Affairs (MCA) has officially confirmed that corporates subscribing to ZCZP instruments issued by eligible registered NPOs can account for it as legitimate CSR spend under Section 135 and Schedule VII.
